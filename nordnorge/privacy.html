Privacy policy

Northern Norway Winter Guide (Nordnorsk Vinterguide) · Last updated: 9 November 2026

The app is a travel guide for winter in Northern Norway. It is built to work on your phone, without an account, and to send as little as possible about you.

1. Who we are

The app is published by RAFIKI - DIGITAL - ELHOLM (Rafiki Digital), a sole proprietorship in Norway, org. no. 938 727 856, 5397 Bekkjarvik, Norway. We are responsible for the processing described here.

2. No account, no server

The app has no user accounts and we run no server that receives data about you. We do not collect, sell or share personal data. The app does not use analytics tools, advertising networks or trackers, and does not show ads inside the app.

3. What is stored on your phone

The following is stored only on your own phone: your settings (language, type of traveller, area, position choice), places and stories you mark with a star, passport stamps (place, time and whether your position matched), photos you add to the passport or postcards, the aurora alarm, whether you have full access, and saved forecasts and map tiles so the app works without internet. Map tiles you have viewed are kept for 30 days.

4. Your location

The app asks for location only while you use it (never in the background). It is used to show the distance to places, the nearest toilet, the aurora chance where you are, and to check that you are at a place when you stamp the passport. The exact position is used on the phone and is never sent to us.

Some parts of the app need data from public services. When they do, the app sends a position as described below:

  • Weather and cloud cover (MET Norway, api.met.no): the position is rounded to two decimals (about 1 km) before it is sent. For «Colourful sky», the app also fetches the cloud forecast for a point about 80 km towards the sun, rounded the same way.
  • Place names and heights in camera navigation (Kartverket, the Norwegian Mapping Authority): you choose in Settings under «Position and Kartverket»: approximate (rounded to about 1 km, the default), precise, or off (nothing is sent).
  • Avalanche warnings (NVE / Varsom), tides (Kartverket) and nearby parking and toilets (OpenStreetMap): the app sends the coordinates of the place you are looking at, not your own position.
  • Aurora activity (NOAA, USA): no position is sent.

The home screen widget and the aurora alarm use the last position the app saved while it was open, or the place you chose for the alarm.

5. Maps

The map uses Google Maps on Android with map tiles from Kartverket. When you open the map, Google and Kartverket receive normal technical information such as your IP address and which map area is loaded. If you allow location, your position is shown on the map by the phone and Google Maps. Google's privacy policy applies to Google Maps: policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Like any internet request

When the app fetches data from the services above, or shows a live webcam image from a camera owner's website, that server can see your phone's IP address and the time, as with any website. The app identifies itself to MET Norway with the app name and our contact address, as MET requires. No name, e-mail or other personal data is sent.

7. Partner links (marked «Ad»)

Buttons for accommodation, tours and car rental are links to partners such as Booking.com (via CJ), Stay22, GetYourGuide and Discover Cars. They are marked «Annonse» (ad) in the app. The link contains our partner ID so the partner can see that you came from the app, and we may earn a small commission if you book. The price is the same for you. When you open a link you leave the app, and the partner's own privacy policy and cookies apply. We do not receive your name, contact details, payment details or what you book, only anonymous totals from the partner.

8. Purchases

Full access is bought as a one-time purchase in Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or account details. The app only stores on the phone that you have full access. Google's privacy policy applies to the purchase.

9. Feedback and ratings

  • «Send a tip or correction»: opens your own e-mail app with a ready message that you can read and change before you send it. It contains what you wrote, which place or page it is about and the app language. If you send it, we receive your e-mail address and the message, and use it only to answer you and improve the guide. We delete it when it is no longer needed.
  • Rating: the rating is given in Google Play through Google's own review window. We only see the public rating and text, as in the store.

10. Permissions

  • Location: see section 4. You can use the app without it, but distances, stamps and «where you are» will not work.
  • Camera: for camera navigation, the starry sky and photos in the passport. Images are not sent anywhere unless you share them yourself.
  • Photos: to choose a photo for the passport or save a postcard to your gallery.
  • Notifications: only for the aurora alarm, if you turn it on.

Read-aloud of stories uses the phone's own speech engine. The app does not use the microphone.

11. Deleting your data

You can remove stars and photos in the app. To delete everything, uninstall the app or clear its storage in the phone settings. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing to delete on our side, except e-mails you have sent us. Ask us and we will delete them.

12. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete personal data about you, and to complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet, datatilsynet.no). For e-mails you send us, the legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering you.

13. Children

The app is a travel guide for a general audience and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect data from children.

14. Safety

The safety advice in the app is general. In an emergency call 112 (police), 113 (ambulance) or 110 (fire). Always check the weather, avalanche warnings and road conditions from official sources before you go.

15. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
