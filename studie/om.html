From handwritten notes to exam practice

StudyNotes is a study app for accounting students in Tanzania. The student photographs a page of notes, or types a few lines from a lecture. The app reads the notes, files them under the right subject and topic, explains the content and turns it into practice questions.

It started with one Bachelor of Accounting student in her third year, with exams in the style of the NBAA. The aim is a patient study partner that works on a cheap phone, with a weak connection, in the student's own language.

What the app does

Photo or text
Take a picture of a notebook page or write a note. Without internet the note is kept on the phone and sent when the connection is back.
Your topics and AI topics
The student can save a note under her own subject and topic with one tap. If she leaves it to the AI, the app chooses, and it always shows clearly which topics are hers and which the AI chose.
Three levels of explanation
Simple, advanced and very advanced, from the basic idea to the standard (IFRS, IAS, ISA, IPSAS) and worked numbers.
Quiz with levels
Multiple-choice questions at easy, medium and hard. The student moves up a level when she gets most of the questions right, and every answer is explained.
Learn from mistakes
Wrong answers come back for review at increasing intervals, and a mistakes page explains what went wrong.
Exam problems
Calculation problems with a full solution and marking points, to practise for written exams.
Ask your notes
Ask a question in English or Kiswahili and get an answer built from the student's own notes, with links to them.
Fill the gaps
The app points out what is missing in the notes and can write a short extra note. AI-written material is always marked as such.
Revise by period
Practise on last week, last month, semester 1 or semester 2, with the student's own semester dates.
Calculator
Currency (TZS, USD, EUR, GBP), VAT at 18 %, withholding tax and mark-up.

Languages

The app is in English, Kiswahili and Norwegian. Explanations can be in Kiswahili while exam questions stay in English, as in the real exams.

Privacy

Notes and photos are stored on our own server in Norway, and each student only sees her own notes. To be analysed, the text and photos are sent to an external AI service (currently DeepSeek). Do not write personal information about other people in the notes. A student can ask us to delete her notes at any time.

Important

The explanations and questions are written by AI and can contain mistakes. Always check against the textbook, the standards and the lecturer before an exam.
