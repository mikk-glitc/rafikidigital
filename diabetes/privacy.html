Privacy policy

Rafiki – Diabetes Logbook (Rafiki wa Kisukari) · Last updated: 3 November 2026

Your health information is personal. This app is built so that it stays with you.

1. Who we are

The app is published by RAFIKI - DIGITAL - ELHOLM (Rafiki Digital), a sole proprietorship in Norway, org. no. 938 727 856, 5397 Bekkjarvik, Norway.

2. What the app stores

The app stores what you type in: blood sugar readings, medicines and insulin doses, meals, activity, weight, HbA1c, blood pressure, ketones, lab results, notes, your profile (for example name, year of birth, type of diabetes) and your settings (language, country, unit, target range, reminders).

All of this is stored only on your own phone. The app has no user accounts and no server. We do not collect, receive, sell or share your health data or personal data.

3. Data that leaves your phone, only when you choose

  • Doctor report (PDF): when you create a report, you choose who to send it to and how (for example e-mail or WhatsApp). The sharing is done by your phone and the app you choose.
  • Backup file: when you make a backup, the file is shared the way you choose (for example to yourself by WhatsApp, e-mail or Google Drive). The file contains your health data, so keep it safe.
  • Feedback: if you choose to send feedback, the app opens your own e-mail or WhatsApp with a ready message that you can read before you send it. It contains only what you wrote, your star rating, which part of the app it is about, the app language, the app version and the type of phone (Android or iPhone). No health data. We use it only to improve the app.

4. Update check

When the app starts, it may read a small public file on this website (version.json) to see if a newer version is available. No personal data or health data is sent. Like any website, the web host can see the phone's IP address for this request.

5. Reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your phone by the phone's own notification system. Nothing is sent to us.

6. Links to AliExpress

The Shop page has links to AliExpress. Some are affiliate links: AliExpress can see that you came from our app and may pay us a small commission if you buy. The price is the same for you. When you open a link you leave our app, and AliExpress's own privacy policy applies. We do not receive your name, address, card details or what you buy.

7. Analytics and advertising

The app does not use analytics tools, advertising networks or trackers, and does not show ads.

8. Permissions

  • Notifications: only for the reminders you turn on.
  • Files: only when you choose a backup file to restore.

9. Deleting your data

You can delete single entries in the logbook. To delete everything, uninstall the app or clear the app's storage in your phone settings. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing to delete on our side.

10. Children

The app is meant for adults and for parents or carers who help a child with diabetes. It does not knowingly collect any data from children. All data stays on the device.

11. Health information

The app gives general health information and is not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat illness and does not replace your health worker. Always talk to your doctor or nurse before you change medicine or insulin.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

13. Contact

Questions about privacy: post.rafiki.digital+privacy@gmail.com
